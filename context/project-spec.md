# Bela Website — Project Spec

## Problem

**What problem are we solving?**

- Bela does multiple things: dancing, singing, acting...
- She needs to showcase her work on the web so that people can have an idea of the work she does
- They will be able to contact her
- Unlike Instagram, which works for only one activity, the website will show many things (compartiments)
- It should attract professionals that would want to work with/for her
- It will show that she is still very active

## Users

**Who is this for?**

- Majoritairement français
- Professional dancers, singers
- Employeur : chorégraphe, metteur en scène, boîte de prod, compagnie de danse
- Partenaires
- Clients : élèves, personne ayant des services comme chorégraphe ou directrice de mouvements

## Features

**What does the MVP need?**

- **Home page:** "Je suis une interprète" — photos and videos of her only
- **Chorégraphe:** photos and videos of her and others that she helped
  - Reference: https://www.joranerest.com/personal-work
- **Singing** page
- **Cabaret** page
- **About:** short bio
- **Languages:** French by default, with English and Portuguese
- **Contact:** email, social platforms, form

## Monetize

**How will this make money?**

- Indirectement avec des embauches sur différents projets : concerts, musiques, spectacles, pièces...
