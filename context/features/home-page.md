# Home page

## Overview

This is the home page for Bela Balsa's portfolio website. It serves as the first impression for visitors, showcasing her work as a professional dancer, singer, and actress. The page should be visually engaging, highlighting her photos and videos to convey her talent and activity in the performing arts.

## Requirements

- Main dashboard layout and any global styles
- Dark mode by default
- Top bar with navigation links to other sections (Chorégraphe, Singing, Cabaret, About, Contact)
- Hero section with a full-bleed photo or video and a tagline ("Je suis une interprète")
- Sections for each of her skills (Chorégraphe, Singing, Cabaret) with links to their respective pages
- Contact section with email, social links, and a contact form
